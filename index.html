Target income

Total household income you want each year from each age. State and defined benefit pensions count towards it first; the pots are drawn down to make up the rest.

Pension pots & savings

Payments in stop when the owner retires. Access from age is the owner's age when the pot can first be drawn from (57 for most pensions from 2028). Leave it blank for ISAs and savings, which can be used any time, so they bridge the gap until the pensions open.
