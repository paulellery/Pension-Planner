People

Ages in the charts and target income follow the first person.

Target income

Total household income you want each year after tax, from each age. State and defined benefit pensions count towards it first; pensions and ISAs are drawn down to make up the rest, using each person's tax-free allowance first.

Pensions

Payments in stop when the owner retires. Access from age is the owner's age when the pension can first be drawn from (57 for most pensions from 2028).

ISAs & savings

Can be drawn from any time, so they bridge the gap until the pensions open. Payments in stop when the owner retires.
